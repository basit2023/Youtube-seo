Privacy Policy

VideoSEO Lab is operated by Abdul Basit and is designed to do its core work inside your browser. This notice explains what the current version processes and saves.

Drafts and form content

Titles, descriptions, keywords, tags, chapters, and comparison drafts are processed in your browser. The workspace may save these fields in your browser’s local storage so they remain available after a refresh. You can remove them with the Reset workspace button or by clearing site data in your browser.

Thumbnail files

Thumbnail images selected in the preview tool are read locally to create an on-screen preview. This version does not upload the image to an application server and does not save the image after the page is closed.

Server logs

The hosting provider may record ordinary technical logs such as IP address, browser details, requested pages, timestamps, and error information for delivery, reliability, and security. These logs are not used to build creator profiles inside the tool.

Analytics, advertising, and cookies

The current product does not include analytics or advertising scripts. Browser local storage is used only to preserve workspace drafts. If advertising or analytics are introduced later, this notice and the required consent controls will be updated before those services are activated.

Third-party platforms

The product may mention YouTube Studio, Google Trends, or other research tools. Following an external link makes the destination’s privacy practices apply. VideoSEO Lab does not control those services.
