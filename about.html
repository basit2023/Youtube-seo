A FastSiteCheck product

Useful guidance without imaginary certainty.

VideoSEO Lab by FastSiteCheck helps creators think clearly about video relevance, packaging, and viewer expectations. It does not sell a secret formula, guarantee rankings, or label made-up numbers as search volume.

Why this product exists

YouTube SEO advice often overstates the importance of tags and undersells the harder work: choosing a useful topic, making an accurate promise, earning the click, and keeping that promise once the video starts. This workspace brings those decisions together without making the creator fight a crowded dashboard.

How scores work

The SEO Studio uses a visible checklist. Points reward clear target intent, natural phrase placement, useful description context, an opening hook, valid chapter structure, restrained tags, and concise thumbnail copy. The score evaluates preparation only. It cannot predict demand, competition, audience satisfaction, or future rankings.
